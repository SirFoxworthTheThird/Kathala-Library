# Artwork and map sources

This edition uses 107 original illustrations generated with Codex's built-in image tool from the text of L. Frank Baum's *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz* (1900). Every visible art slot has a different image. The art follows the book's own details: Dorothy's silver shoes and blue-and-white checked dress, the West Witch's one powerful eye without green skin, Glinda's red hair and white dress, and the book's locations and journey.

The 54 public-domain W. W. Denslow illustrations used in the previous edition are no longer referenced or shipped. Their Wikimedia Commons citations and the original map provenance are preserved in `scripts/the-wonderful-wizard-of-oz-art/ORIGINAL-SOURCES.md`; the original blob records are preserved in `original-sources.json` beside it.

Nine original painted map illustrations were generated with the built-in image tool and stored as JPEGs: the Oz overview, Kansas, the Emerald City, Oz's palace, the Yellow Castle, the China Country, Glinda's castle, and the eastern and southern journeys. Each has a distinct image. The 55 interactive locations were placed on the finished paintings so their pins align with visible landmarks. The geography follows the first book; exact distances, buildings, and interior layouts are editorial interpretations because Baum published no measured map with the 1900 book. Map prompts and placement notes are in `scripts/the-wonderful-wizard-of-oz-art/MAP-PROMPTS.md`.

Generation, prompts, contact sheets, import and validation are documented in `scripts/the-wonderful-wizard-of-oz-art/`.
