# Sources and provenance

The complete manuscript follows John Buchan’s original English novel *The Thirty-Nine Steps*, first published in 1915, using Project Gutenberg eBook #558. The exact UTF-8 source file was updated 7 April 2021 and credits Jo Churcher, HTML work by Al Haines, and corrections by Menno de Leeuw:

- https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/558
- https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/558/pg558.txt

Project Gutenberg identifies the ebook as public domain in the United States. There is no translator. The archived source SHA-256 is `8e13489506fd26a159c1bed66d3bd0f0d485083ee53effcfa1f1f2542852459f` after line endings are normalized to LF.

Extraction begins with the dedication to Thomas Arthur Nelson and ends after the final narrative paragraph. It preserves the dedication, its September 1915 date, all ten author-supplied chapter headings, and every narrative word in order. Exclusions are limited to the Gutenberg header, production metadata, initial illustration placeholder, duplicate title/author wrapper, contents list, END delimiter, and licence.

All structural summaries, event titles and descriptions, chronology between explicitly dated points, map interpretation, and supporting records are original editorial work. The novel fixes 24 May, 31 May, 12 June, 14 June, and 15 June; its last paragraph places the action seven weeks before Britain entered the First World War. The other event times are documented approximations.

All 77 images are original assets generated separately for this example with OpenAI’s built-in image generation tool on 15 September 2026. They are documented in `scripts/the-thirty-nine-steps/ARTWORK.md`. The PWK uses repository-relative images and needs no PWB bundle.
