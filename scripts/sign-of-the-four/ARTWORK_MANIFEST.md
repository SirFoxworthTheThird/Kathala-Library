# Artwork manifest — The Sign of the Four

All 67 accepted assets are original images generated separately with OpenAI imagegen for this edition on 4–9 October 2026. They were not sourced from a third-party image collection. Every accepted PNG was opened for visual inspection. Maps were generated as navigable cartographic images, not reused as ordinary location art. The final file paths below are within `library/sign-of-the-four/`.

The original generation prompts for the cover, maps and first 18 portraits were recovered from the Codex session record. Each row for those portraits completes the shared prefix exactly. Later rows reproduce their individual prompts. The provenance for each accepted image is `OpenAI imagegen`, generated specifically for the Kathala Library edition.

## Corrections and asset integrity

Four first-pass Lodge images and one first-pass masked accomplice portrait were rejected after visual inspection; their replacements are listed above. The rejected images were never copied into the Library. The original prompt for the rejected accomplice began `A mysterious small barefoot figure seen from behind on the deck of a Victorian Thames steam launch at night` and requested a concealed face, but produced a long-haired, gown-like silhouette; the final prompt made the intended adult male silhouette explicit.

The book validator checks that every referenced image file exists and that no accepted images share a SHA-256 digest. `ARTWORK_MANIFEST.md` lists one final image per cover, map, character, item and location entity.
