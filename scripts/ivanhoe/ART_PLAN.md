# Ivanhoe artwork plan

Status: inventory IN PROGRESS; no complete coverage claim.

Use original, asset-specific illustrated imagery. Keep medieval material culture
and the novel's own dress/objects distinct from modern film versions. Avoid
caricature, fantasy armour, recognisable film likenesses, text and watermarks.
Common art direction: detailed painterly historical book plates, subdued
earth/forest colours, readable subjects at card size, humane individual faces.

## First asset

- ID: cover. Source basis: chapter VIII paragraphs 40–47, anonymous armoured
  champion on a black horse, shield with uprooted young oak. Keep face concealed
  and omit a legible inscription; no royal heraldry or later identities.
- Built-in image generation. Save exact prompt with asset manifest.
- Original, compressed output and in-application presentation each require
  separate visual review. Do not count a generated output as integrated.

## Coverage still to inventory

All required cast, locations, objects and navigational layers. Candidates from
the first half include Yorkshire/Midlands topography, Rotherwood including
raised chamber and guest cells, Ashby town/field and tournament enclosure,
Copmanhurst chapel/hut/fountain, and Torquilstone moat, barbican, court, old
and new halls, deep dungeon and isolated turret balcony. These are source
requirements, not a completed map list or permission to omit later places.

Torquilstone's turret balcony is isolated: do not draw a walkable connection
to neighbouring battlements. The old hall's windows face the court. The
Ashby castle is the earlier building, not Hastings's later surviving ruins.
Concealed cupboards in Copmanhurst must not disclose their contents through
first-seen location artwork before those contents are revealed.
