# Ivanhoe item review

The source inventory contains55 individually illustrated objects and mounts.
The current compiler records66 source actions,92 item states,6 placements and5
reported relinquishments. Its source witnesses are in compile-inventory.mjs;
the earlier custodyEvidence ledger records the decisions behind the principal
handoffs. Final application approval is still separate from this source review.

Inventory means the last recorded possession, not a claim that every object is
in a person's hands in every later scene. Do not invent disposal, repayment,
transport or destruction merely because an object stops being mentioned. The
reader can inspect the source state and its moment. Unknown destinations remain
unknown; a horse in an inventory is a recorded mount, not an indoor map occupant.

| Athelstane's battle-axe; black-armoured rider's tournament sword; John's warder | XII.35 is divided into actual action phases. The sword breaks; the rescuer wrenches the axe away; John later throws his warder. Neither later axe disposal nor an exact warder landing place is narrated. |
| Gurth's collar, horn, whittle and quarterstaff | I.9 establishes the worn collar and belt equipment; the staff is picked up at I.29. XXXII.20 changes the collar's meaning when Gurth is freed. His request for a smith and file is not a witnessed cutting-off ceremony. The compiler now records that condition explicitly. |
| Wamba's collar, wooden sword, steel falchion and target | The collar and lath blade belong to the opening description. The real sword is taken during XIX.20; the target is explicitly described at XL.33. The wooden sword's replacement is reported there, without inventing its destination. Wamba is not freed with Gurth. |
| Prize bugle, archer's baldric, cap feather and vizard | XIII awards the horn; XIX.38 gives the horn, belt and feather to Wamba while Locksley takes out his mask. XXXII.53 establishes the horn back with Locksley without narrating the intervening return. The later gift to the Black Knight therefore clears Wamba's obsolete holding through a report. XL.80 explicitly hands horn and belt back to Wamba. |
| Aymer's collar | The wager is not a completed delivery. It remains his last recorded possession; do not manufacture payment of the wine wager. |
| Brian's pledge-chain; Palmer's reliquary | The pledge and custody with Aymer/Ambrose follow V.48–54. XXXV.54 reports the chain on an unnamed deer-stalker's neck. Neither Locksley nor the friar is identified as its recipient. XLIII's recalled challenge does not put either pledge object on the combat field. |
| Palmer's staff; Isaac's credit order | IV.6 directly describes the staff. VI.86 delivers the written credit order; the offer of later equipment does not itself complete an equipment handoff. No later order delivery or staff disposal is invented. |
| Borrowed horse and gold-inlaid armour; prize horse | The anonymous challenger actually enters with the borrowed equipment at VIII.40 and receives the distinct prize horse at IX.18. X.48 explicitly reports the borrowed horse restored to Isaac's stable. It does not state that the armour was returned. No identity between these horses and later unidentified mounts is invented. |
| Gurth's reward coins; master's gold bag; acquittance; Rebecca's purse | X distinguishes personal reward, entrusted money, payment receipt and the hundred-piece gift purse. XI's seizure, failed recovery attempt and later spared release are separate changes. No unreported repayment to the master or additional piles of coins are created. |
| Chaplet; John's foreign billet | The chaplet is awarded before the victor's name is spoken. The billet passes successively through John's advisers; De Bracy is the last named recipient. Its contents disclose the king's release, not the Black Knight's concealed identity. |
| Friar's frock, cowl and cord; Cedric's cloak and girdle | Wamba's completed disguise precedes his completed exchange with Cedric. The cord is directly worn at XXVI.3. A later mention of a friar wearing a frock does not establish that it is the same garment or narrate its return from Cedric. |
| Hut key | XX.61 places it beneath the threshold. It is not retained in the hermit's pocket. |
| Defiance; reply; Malvoisin dispatch; Athelstane's glove | The compiler follows the actual last recipients. Unnamed messengers are not identified as familiar named characters. Cedric's intended dispatch delivery is not performed in the narrative. The Baron's command to hang the glove does not prove a completed placement. |
| De Bracy's sword; Ulrica's distaff | XXXI.52 surrenders the sword to Locksley; release of the prisoner is not a narrated weapon restoration. The distaff is brandished on the turret and lost when it falls; no recovery is invented. |
| Rebecca's balsam; Higg's medicine box | Rebecca's retained phial and the old box she gave Higg are distinct objects. The retrospective treatment is not a fresh wound. The Master confiscates the box at XXXVII.26; that does not confiscate Rebecca's phial. |
| Aymer's tablets | Isaac supplies them, Aymer writes and returns them, and Conrade later reads them after the Master opens them. The tablets are not Higg's later Hebrew appeal. |
| Champion warning; Hebrew appeal | The warning is thrust into Rebecca's hand before its contents or sender are disclosed, then destroyed. Brian's later admission does not recreate it. The separate appeal passes to Higg; Isaac collapses after seeing it, and Ben Samuel reads it. The source does not establish whose hand retains it during the collapse. |
| Rebecca's glove | Cast down, taken by the Master, retained by Albert despite the alleged acceptance, placed at the Master's feet and finally thrown into the lists. Brian's supposed acceptance is not an actual handoff to him. |
| Malkin; Black Knight's charger; Ivanhoe's war-horse; Zamor | The Prior's jennet is explicitly lent; Gurth is later seen riding the distinct war-horse. The black charger's mortal wound makes it unusable after XL.99. Brian names his current horse during the proposed escape. The exhausted challenger mount is not automatically identified as one of the earlier horses. |
| Fetter key | Athelstane reports using the sacristan's key in his escape. He does not say he kept it; the account creates no present acquisition in the guest room. |
| Silver casket; diamond necklace; diamond earrings | XLIV.70 offers the casket; its contents first appear when it is opened at71. Rowena handles it and Rebecca urges her to keep it. No separately spoken acceptance or completed return is invented. |

Ordinary cups, food, anonymous soldiers' weapons, architectural fittings and
undifferentiated loot remain in the complete manuscript. They do not acquire
invented individual identities or custody chains. Named mounts, consequential
documents, exchanged disguises, gages and recurring distinctive equipment have
individual records. This selection avoids padding the item roster with props
whose histories the source never distinguishes.

All92 compiled item states were opened and manually reviewed in four records (qa/custody-manual-review-01 through04.json). Five reported relinquishments and five rewind states were separately inspected after the companion item-history fix. Current candidate transition proofs retain the same custody model and image bytes. Final packaged-file validation is recorded separately in LOCAL_RELEASE.json. Zero conflicting carriers alone is not the manual evidence.
