# Character-status wording review

The snapshot ledger removes standing-role sentences and arc summaries from
character statuses. It does not certify the legacy scene model as a faithful
chapter-by-chapter edition. The world has no retained novel text.

## Evidence and limits

Concrete actions in the existing scene descriptions provide the starting
evidence. Where those descriptions are placeholders, Jo Walton's chapter
reread for Tor.com (now Reactor) provides chapter context. The replacement
notes are short editorial paraphrases, not text from the novel or the reread.
The reread sometimes uses draft chapter numbers: later entries give both
numbers. Match titles as well as numbers.

## Separate legacy model defects requiring a source-led edition audit

- `Trust` has only an arc placeholder as its synopsis; the sympathy duel belongs
  there. The current `The Crucible` synopsis instead describes that duel but its
  cast omits Devi. The status edit must not be mistaken for a corrected synopsis.
- `Stolen` describes recovery of Denna's ring, although the chapter concerns
  Kvothe's missing lute. Its new status concerns the missing lute.
- `Without Word or Warning` describes departure even though the chapter is
  still in the University sequence before `The Catch` and `Consortation`.
- Several later descriptions mix events between adjacent chapters, and some
  casts omit a person central to the scene description or retain people from an
  earlier scene. `Interlude—Fences`, for example, retains the four mercenaries
  alongside the Waystone participants. The revised wording identifies the
  mercenaries as subjects of the paused account, not people at the inn.
- Chapter titles/numbers in the Ademre sequence need comparison with the
  published edition before making claims about exact chronology.

These synopsis, cast, and chapter-boundary issues are recorded rather than
silently changing scene membership, chronology, or narrative content during
the status wording repair. They remain open; passing the wording tests does not
resolve them.
