# The Odyssey artwork review

The initial replacement contained 31 distinct original illustrations. This follow-up adds 78 new illustrations so that all 109 illustrated slots have distinct art. The four map layers remain linked for geographic reference. The Penelope interview painting now belongs to Penelope's portrait; her brooch item has its own close-up.

## Visual direction and review

The set uses a consistent Bronze Age Aegean setting, painterly tempera and fine ink, and Aegean blue, terracotta, ivory and muted gold. The 78 additions were reviewed in batches at full size. Menelaus was regenerated to distinguish him from Odysseus; the olive-wood stake and the underworld sacrifice pit were regenerated to remove graphic details and better match the text. The original bow scene had already been regenerated during the first replacement.

The first 31 prompts are recorded in `PROMPTS.md`; the added prompts and per-slot status are recorded in `unique-art-plan.json`. The new compact JPEGs are committed. `validate.mjs` checks 109 distinct artwork references and blobs, four maps, resolved image references, JPEG integrity, unique file hashes, source archive and catalogue byte count.
