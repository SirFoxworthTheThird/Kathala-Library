# Map source and visual review

The five geographic charts are drawn from [Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson). Natural Earth [places these vector data in the public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). The exact downloaded GeoJSON is `map-source/ne_110m_land.geojson`, 138,160 bytes, SHA-256 `9e0729ee253ca7d7a5c4ae9395fb1902264c5377c52e224d13dd85010e2835d9`. The renderer uses equirectangular longitude/latitude within each chart's stated extent. The 1497–1499 sea track is an editorial approximation from the voyage journal cited in `CHRONOLOGY.md`, not a surviving ship's plotted log. It deliberately does not draw the poem's allegorical island at a real coordinate.

The source data and `render-maps.py` reproduce the eight PNGs. `map-marker-coordinates.json` records pixels with **y measured from the bottom edge**, as Kathala requires; the renderer computes those from WGS84 coordinates, or from declared schematic positions for the three invented maps. The master ocean chart spans longitude −28° to 88°, latitude −43° to 48°; Portugal, East Africa and Malabar are regional charts. England supports Veloso's embedded tale. The Calicut city chart is a relative plan of narrated places and is not a surveyed reconstruction. The Island of Love and divine settings are conceptual plans, not geographical assertions.

## Visual inspection, 10 October 2026

All eight final PNGs were opened. The first render revealed border overlap with headings and captions, plus dense labels at the Cape, Mombasa/Melinde, and Calicut. The renderer was corrected and the outputs regenerated. A later spoiler review removed place names that the poem has not yet disclosed from the static PNGs; Kathala’s gated markers must supply those names during reading. The final renders have legible title and caption panels, coastlines, routes, and plotted site dots. The three schematic maps use generic site labels or leave future domains unnamed. Specific expected marker coordinates are listed in `map-marker-coordinates.json` and the artwork manifest.

The local Kathala development UI was then checked at `http://127.0.0.1:5173/` with the generated PWK imported in reading mode. Each of the eight layers was selected and visually inspected at the default useful zoom: the ocean road, Portugal and the Tagus, East African coast, Malabar approach, Calicut narrated places, England and Flanders, the Island of Love, and divine settings. The coastlines, plotted route, site markers, and conceptual labels rendered in their expected regions. The ocean map exposes the Lisbon, Mozambique coast, and Calicut coast gateways. The location panel's **Open Sub-map** action successfully entered the Portugal, East Africa, and Malabar layers; the Calicut marker on Malabar entered the nested city layer. The map playback control advanced automatically across layers, including Portugal and Calicut, without a browser console error. Dense character overlays can obscure the gateway marker; turning off the Characters overlay makes the underlying location selectable. Pixel bounds and bottom-edge coordinates are also checked by `validate-maps.mjs` and `validate-book.mjs`.
