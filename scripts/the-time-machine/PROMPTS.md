# The Time Machine generated-art directives

## Shared direction

Naturalistic oil-painted late-Victorian book illustration. The 1890s Richmond scenes use dark wood, umber, forest green, brass, nickel, and ivory; the year 802,701 uses weathered pale stone, lush overgrowth, and unsettling sunset gold; the further future becomes spare and red. Keep the Traveller, his brass-and-ivory saddle-like machine, the White Sphinx, Eloi, and Morlocks visually consistent. Give the physically undescribed Victorian guests distinct age, build, hair, dress, and expression as editorial interpretations. Depict Eloi and Morlocks as human descendants, without caricature or graphic violence. No readable text, logos, signatures, watermarks, modern electronics, or generic steampunk decoration.

Each asset was generated separately with its own subject directive. The contact sheets record the reviewed final images.
