# Scene boundary audit

The 130 scenes use only retained paragraph boundaries. `BOUNDARY_AUDIT.json` records every chapter and scene number, title, narrative change, location, entering and leaving cast, individual status notes, modeled knowledge and item hand-offs, inclusive paragraph range, adjacent paragraph excerpts, and the SHA-256 of the exact scene prose. The ranges cover paragraphs 1 through the last paragraph of every chapter with no gaps or overlap. All 130 opening and closing paragraphs were compared with their adjacent paragraphs and current scene descriptions. Source-wide sweeps of attributed speech, frequent character mentions, and travel or arrival language were used to reopen the cast and location decisions; their candidates were resolved against the retained prose rather than added as automatic presence.

This review moved the Chapter V cottage arrival and Great House visit to their source paragraphs; separated the Crofts’ Chapter VI visit from earlier Uppercross gatherings; cut the Chapter VIII discussion when the Admiral joins Wentworth; added the Chapter IX cottage arrivals; corrected cast for the Chapter XV accident account, Chapter XVI Dalrymple discussion, and Chapter XVIII letter reading; and moved the Chapter XXIII Harville cut to his invitation before the miniature is shown. The Chapter XII morning encounter occurs on Lyme’s sands, so its scene points to Lyme Regis rather than the Cobb. The three material objects and five modeled knowledge reveals are assigned at the paragraphs that first support them.

Cuts follow changes in place, company, immediate action, or narrative purpose. Letters and reported histories remain inside the physical scene where they are read or told. Chapter IV is modeled as Anne’s present reflection at Kellynch; Lady Russell, Sir Walter, and Wentworth are mentioned within that recollection and receive no physical-presence snapshot there. Chapter XXI keeps Mrs Smith’s account in Westgate Buildings. Each event’s `involvedCharacterIds` has exactly one matching snapshot per modeled physical participant, and no snapshot is generated from `mentionedCharacterIds`.

Chapter XXIV is narrator-led aftermath, with no physically staged gathering and no character snapshots. Its Bath location is an editorial geographic anchor for map navigation; it does not assert that every reported later action happens in Bath. The same convention applies to the setting markers on earlier narrator-led passages with empty cast.
