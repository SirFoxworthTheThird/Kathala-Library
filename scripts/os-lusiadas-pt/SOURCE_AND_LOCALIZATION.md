# Os Lusíadas — Portuguese reading entry

This is a second catalogue entry for the same complete poem. The source, publication and rights record, extraction boundaries, retained-text normalization, canto and stanza counts, scene-cut ledger, chronology, and coordinate audit are in the adjacent `scripts/os-lusiadas/` directory: `SOURCE.md`, `BOUNDARY_REVIEW.md`, `CHRONOLOGY.md`, and `MAP_REVIEW.md`. The source is the original 1572 Portuguese text transcribed in Project Gutenberg eBook 27236. The poem is public domain. This entry does not translate or modernize a verse: its 367 scene passages match the original entry byte for byte, comprising 10 cantos, 1,102 stanzas, 8,816 verse lines and 55,283 whitespace-delimited words.

The new editorial language is European Portuguese. Scene titles and summaries, character and place names and descriptions, item and faction labels, knowledge, motifs, goals, timeline and map labels, and Lore pages were localized. Editorial spelling uses contemporary European Portuguese, while the 1572 transcription keeps its historic spelling, long s, punctuation, and canto headings. The Google Translate pt-PT endpoint supplied an initial draft for 2,320 metadata fields; `translations.json` freezes that draft. `reviewed-phrases.json` and `overrides.json` record editorial corrections, especially historical names, nautical terms, narrating frames, interpretive cartography, and source notices. The shipped generator is offline: `node scripts/os-lusiadas-pt/generate.mjs` applies the frozen data with source-drift and orphan-override checks. This is an editorial localization of metadata, not a new translation of Camões's poem.

The existing 158 non-map illustrations are intentionally reused. Eight maps are rendered anew with Portuguese titles and labels using `python scripts/os-lusiadas/render-maps.py --pt`; they use the same geographic extents, Natural Earth relief/coastline sources, route and marker coordinates as the original topographic edition. Five chart layers depict real geography approximately; the Calecute site plan, Ilha dos Amores and divine setting use explicitly interpretive terrain. `scripts/os-lusiadas-pt/validate.mjs` checks all localized structure against the original after restoring locale-specific fields and IDs, every exact verse passage, catalogue bytes and counts, map dimensions, and all 166 image paths. The original source validator independently checks manuscript reconstruction and stanza boundaries.

## UI review, 11 October 2026

The exact generated PWK was imported in Kathala development UI at `http://127.0.0.1:5173/` in reading mode. Its Portuguese title, introduction and opening verse appeared; unrevealed characters and places remained gated. The development app did not serve newly added Library-worktree map assets from its own `/library/` path before publication. For visual map QA, a temporary copy of that PWK changed only its world IDs and asset base URLs to a local static server on port 5174. It was imported as a separate QA world. Advancing to the first voyage scene loaded its ocean map; all eight map layers were opened and displayed with Portuguese titles and image labels. Book reading, Characters, Calendar, Items, Relations, Arc, Lore, Factions, and Knowledge were opened; the browser error log returned `[]`. This asset-host substitution tests the new PNGs in Kathala but does not claim that the development app already serves unpublished Library paths. The exact PWK's 166 relative paths resolve within this repository and are checked by the dedicated validator.
