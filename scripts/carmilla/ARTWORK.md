# Carmilla artwork record

All 49 final images were generated separately with the built-in image-generation tool: one cover, four navigable maps, sixteen portraits, twelve item studies, and sixteen location illustrations. No external artwork is used and no image bytes are duplicated.

Shared direction: mature nineteenth-century Gothic engraving fused with restrained oil wash; charcoal, muted ivory, oxblood, forest green, and silver; historically plausible dress, architecture, and objects; no modern interfaces, logos, signatures, or watermarks. Maps were requested as top-down navigational aids rather than scenes.

Every final was opened during generation and checked for subject, period, anatomy, legibility, category, and stylistic consistency. No generated attempt was rejected after inspection; filenames follow the entity keys in `generate.mjs`.
