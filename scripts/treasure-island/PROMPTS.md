# Treasure Island generated-art directives

## Shared direction

Naturalistic oil-painted mid-eighteenth-century British maritime adventure illustration with restrained period engraving texture. Use coherent sea green, weathered blue, ochre, salt-stained wood, and worn leather. Keep Jim adolescent; make Silver one-legged with a crutch, charismatic and watchful; distinguish professional officers, loyal servants, and individual mutineers without caricature. The Hispaniola is a modest two-masted schooner throughout. Keep Bristol, the English coast, ship interiors, and the island distinct. Island scenes repeat wooded Spy-glass Hill, the eastern anchorage and marsh, North Inlet, and the stockade consistently. No readable text, logos, signatures, watermarks, modern equipment, fantasy architecture, cartoon pirates, or graphic violence.

Each output was generated separately using the shared direction and the asset-specific directive below. Contact sheets show the reviewed final images.
