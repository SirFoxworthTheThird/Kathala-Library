# Prompt set

All 126 images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, one call per distinct asset. The individual subject and purpose of each image are recorded by slot in `manifest.json`. Prompts used the following shared direction and a scene description tailored to the slot:

> Naturalistic literary ink and watercolor on textured paper, historically grounded in the early nineteenth century. Restrained sea blue, limestone, ochre, and umber. No readable text, logo, watermark, collage, modern objects, or film likenesses.

- **Cover:** Mature Edmond Dantès on a rocky Mediterranean shore at twilight, Château d'If and a ship across the water, vertical composition with clear upper sky.
- **Characters:** One recognizable person in a waist-up three-quarter portrait, with clothing and a lightly suggested setting tied to the person's role and period.
- **Items:** A single object or tight narrative still life, with no readable writing on letters or documents.
- **Locations:** A landscape establishing view focused on architecture and geography; people appear only as small contextual figures.
- **Lore:** A symbolic editorial scene distinct from the specific character, item, and location views.
- **Factions:** A wide collective scene expressing the faction's social role and setting, without using a single character portrait as its cover.

The per-slot prompts specified the subjects explicitly, including separate views for Château d'If, its landing stair, cells, tunnel, ramparts, and burial sea; Monte Cristo island, its cove, goat path, treasure cave, and grotto; and each Paris household and office.
