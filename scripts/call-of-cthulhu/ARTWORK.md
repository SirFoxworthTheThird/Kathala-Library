# The Call of Cthulhu artwork record

All 46 images were generated originally for this world: one cover, four navigable maps, eleven character portraits, nine item studies, and twenty-one location illustrations. No external artwork is used.

Shared direction: mature 1920s weird-fiction engraving fused with muted oil colour; charcoal, aged ivory, and sea-green palette; historically plausible clothing and objects; no modern interfaces, logos, signatures, or watermarks. Maps were prompted as labeled top-down navigational aids and visually reviewed separately from decorative art.

Each filename matches its entity key in `world-ledger.mjs`. Generation prompts specified the named person, object, place, or map independently; no image bytes are reused.
