# A Tale of Two Cities generated-art directives

## Shared direction

Naturalistic late-eighteenth-century oil-painted realism with restrained Victorian book-illustration texture. Expressive, unsentimental characterisation and historically grounded urban atmosphere. London uses muted browns and river greys; Paris uses pale stone, smoke, wine-dark reds, and restrained revolutionary tricolour accents. Show plausible clothing, hairstyles, architecture, courts, prisons, roads, transport, and domestic work between 1775 and the French Revolution. No readable text, logos, signatures, watermarks, graphic violence, or modern objects.

Each image was generated separately. These asset-specific directives record subject, identity, and narrative function; contact sheets record the reviewed outputs.
