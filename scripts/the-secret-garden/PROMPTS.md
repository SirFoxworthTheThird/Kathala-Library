# The Secret Garden generated-art prompts

All 50 assets were generated separately with asset-specific prompts. Shared direction: naturalistic oil-painted realism, refined Edwardian and Golden Age children’s-book texture, historically plausible circa-1900–1911 clothing, architecture, interiors, tools, transport, horticulture, and domestic work, with no readable text, logos, signatures, or watermarks.

The cover and 12 character prompts specify age, health, class, family role, temperament, clothing, and distinct settings. Mary remains a thin serious ten-year-old; Colin a pale, deconditioned but not deformed ten-year-old; Dickon a robust twelve-year-old moor boy. Adult labor, grief, medicine, and colonial hierarchy are depicted without caricature. Mary’s ayah is treated as an individual worker rather than decorative colonial scenery.

Eight object prompts specify period materials and function. Twenty-nine location prompts trace India, the sea voyage, England, Lake Como, Yorkshire, Misselthwaite’s estate and service spaces, and the garden’s progress from dormancy to restoration while maintaining architectural and geographic continuity.
