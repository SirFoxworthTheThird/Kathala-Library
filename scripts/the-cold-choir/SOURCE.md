## Normalization and reconstruction

`source-text.mjs` decodes UTF-8, converts CRLF to LF, removes trailing horizontal whitespace, collapses runs of three or more line breaks to one blank line, removes surplus terminal line breaks, and restores exactly one final LF. The dedicated validator reconstructs every chapter heading, section heading, and retained section body in source order, then compares that result with the normalized archived source. It separately compares all scene text with the retained narrative and checks chapter, section, scene, and word counts.

## Structure and chronology

The manuscript alternates among three explicitly dated eras: *The Mercy* in 1 Before Landfall, *The Compact* in 310–311 After Landfall, and *The Quiet* in 742 After Landfall. These are modeled as distinct timelines. Recovered records, inherited objects, and recounted history connect them; their events are not flattened into a single misleading voyage chronology.
