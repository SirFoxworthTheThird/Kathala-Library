# Editorial chronology

The novel supplies seasons, ages and elapsed years but no complete dated calendar. `calendar-model.mjs` uses 365-day editorial years, with early June of year zero at day 152. It claims no historical start year. The final scene is day 2010.95: 1858.95 days after the opening, approximately five years.

Intermediate days are editorial approximations of school terms, seasons, journeys and reported intervals. They are not recovered dates. Fractions order successive actions without claiming clock-time precision. Exact scene anchors are committed in the calendar model; the generator rejects time regression rather than silently clamping it.

There is one narrative timeline. Anne's childhood account is the drive during which she tells Marilla; the entrance-examination letter is Anne writing at Beechwood. Reported people receive no presence snapshots in those scenes. Past households and examination-room actions are not relocated into the current scene or assigned invented dated reenactments.
