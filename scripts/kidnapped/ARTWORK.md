# Artwork manifest

All 73 final PNG assets below `library/kidnapped/` were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generator on 21 September 2026. The set contains one world image, twenty character portraits, twelve item studies, thirty-five location illustrations, and five maps. Every entity has a separate file and distinct byte hash; no map doubles as entity artwork.

## Prompt direction and provenance

Every asset was generated separately. Character, item, location, and world prompts shared this direction:

> Mature literary illustration for Robert Louis Stevenson's *Kidnapped*, in the visual language of a finely printed late-Victorian historical-adventure edition: realist drawing, restrained ink and oil colour, atmospheric eighteenth-century Scotland, dignified composition, period-appropriate clothing, tools, ships, and architecture, no interface, photograph, cartoon styling, modern object, signature, or watermark.

Each prompt then named one subject and supplied its story-supported age, dress, setting, action, or use. Characters were deliberately differentiated by age, build, class, occupation, Highland or Lowland dress, and temperament. Items were isolated as readable object studies. Locations depicted the named road, room, ship compartment, island, inn, moor, cave, or city without substituting a portrait or map. The world image is an atmospheric Highland journey scene, not cartography.

The five map prompts shared this direction:

> Genuine overhead cartographic artwork for a mature eighteenth-century Scottish travel atlas, engraved linework and restrained watercolour on aged cream paper, geographically coherent terrain or a usable architectural plan, no people, prose labels, interface, scene-painting perspective, signature, or watermark.

- `scotland.png`: an interpretive east-to-west journey chart connecting Essendean, Edinburgh and Shaws, the Forth, Mull, Appin, Rannoch, and the return route.
- `shaws.png`: an invented top-down estate and tower plan with yard, kitchen, dangerous stair, chamber, woods, and approach.
- `covenant.png`: an invented three-deck brig plan with deck, forecastle, hold, cabin, galley, and round-house.
- `highlands.png`: an interpretive Appin-to-Balquhidder flight chart with coast, heather, Corrynakiegh, Rannoch, Ben Alder, and passes.
- `cluny.png`: an invented cave-refuge plan with concealed approach, narrow entrance, and inhabited chamber.

## Review and corrections

Every final asset was opened and visually inspected for subject relevance, mature style, legibility, anatomy, material culture, and consistency. The first `round-house` attempt incorrectly depicted a stone building on land; it was rejected and replaced with the stern cabin of an eighteenth-century brig. That rejected output was not copied into the repository. The final validator confirms 73 existing images and 73 distinct SHA-256 hashes. No external or retrieved artwork is used.
