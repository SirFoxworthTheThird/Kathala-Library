# Inferno map review

Reviewed 2026-10-18 against the six final 1536 × 1024 PNGs.

Kathala stores marker y from the **bottom edge**. Every coordinate in `generate.mjs` was checked in that coordinate system, remains inside the image bounds, and was positioned on the illustrated terrain rather than an ornamental margin.

## Map roles

- **The Descent Through Hell** is a geological cutaway. Its surface markers sit above the cone; Dis, Malebolge, and Cocytus descend on the central axis.
- **Upper Hell** follows the visible descending terraces from the gate at upper left to the red walls of Dis at lower right.
- **Dis and the Circle of Violence** follows the cemetery, broken descent, river, wood, sand, and cataract visible in the relief.
- **Malebolge** uses a radial route from the outer rim toward the central well. The earlier diagonal draft was rejected because it did not follow the final concentric image.
- **Cocytus and the Earth’s Centre** moves from the outer ice toward the central shaft, then turns to the right-hand hidden ascent. The earlier diagonal draft was rejected after visual comparison.
- **Italy and the Western Sea** uses recognizable coastlines and terrain: Rimini and Montefeltro in the Adriatic region, Mantua north of the Po, Pisa and Rome along the peninsula, the Mediterranean south of Europe, and the western voyage beyond Gibraltar.

## Gateways and transitions

The overview contains exactly four gateways: Mouth of Hell → Upper Hell, City of Dis → Lower Hell, Malebolge → Malebolge, and Cocytus → Cocytus. Each child map has one overview gateway. Three additional reading-path transitions connect Dis Gate → Lower Hell, Cataract → Malebolge, and Tenth Bolgia → Cocytus. The history map remains independent because it represents embedded memories rather than a floor of the descent.

Routes were redrawn to meet the final marker centres on Malebolge and Cocytus. Playback order follows source order; embedded histories stay on their own timeline and map.
