# The Name of the Wind artwork review

This replacement contains 23 distinct original illustrations for the 23 externally linked non-map image records in the PWK. Their existing image IDs remain in place so linked locations, characters, items, lore and factions continue to resolve. The five map layers and the separate `.pwb` map and portrait bundle are preserved.

## Visual review

All 23 PNG masters were inspected on two contact sheets for subject, composition, style and unwanted text. The set uses original ink and watercolor scenes in indigo, amber, burgundy and moss green. The draccus was regenerated after review because the initial creature had wings; the approved version is ground dwelling. The affected contact sheet was rebuilt and the final draccus image checked before conversion.

Prompts are recorded in `PROMPTS.md`. PNG masters are local review artifacts; the compact JPEGs and contact sheets are committed. `validate.mjs` checks image references across PWK and PWB, 23 local JPEGs, five maps, unique image hashes, the source archive and both catalogue byte counts.
