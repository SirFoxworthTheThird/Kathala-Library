## Editorial conventions

The source is a modernized twelve-book presentation. It uses modern capitalization and punctuation, typographic apostrophes and quotation marks, and contractions such as “th’”. It does not reproduce the original 1674 typography, long s, page furniture, or line numbering. Verse lines are retained in source order with their source spelling, capitalization, punctuation, and leading indentation. Blank packaging lines are normalized away between retained verse lines.

## Extraction boundaries

The retained poem begins with the heading `Book I` and its first verse, “Of Man’s first disobedience, and the fruit”. It ends with the final verse of Book XII, “Through Eden took their solitary way.” The twelve authorial book headings are structural metadata; every verse line between them is retained.

Excluded packaging is limited to the Project Gutenberg header and licence, title and contents pages, the volunteer introduction, blank page furniture, the post-poem `Transcriber’s Notes`, and the Project Gutenberg footer. None is part of Milton’s poem.

The validator reconstructs the complete retained verse under this normalization: CRLF and CR become LF, trailing whitespace is removed, empty packaging lines between verse lines are ignored, and the resulting text is trimmed once at the outer boundaries. No spelling, punctuation, word, or verse-line normalization is allowed.
